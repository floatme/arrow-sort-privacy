Arrow Sort Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026
Developer contact: adam.segev@gmail.com

Arrow Sort (“the App”) is a free puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the App.

Information we collect

The App does not require an account and does not intentionally collect your name, email address, or contact list. Game progress (level unlocks, streaks, hint/undo balances, and purchase status) is stored locally on your device.

Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and process device identifiers (including advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device/browser information, and interaction data with ads in order to serve, measure, and personalize advertising. You can learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob help.

On Android, you may reset or limit your advertising ID in system settings. In the EEA/UK/Switzerland, a consent form may be shown before personalized ads are requested.

Analytics

If crash or usage analytics are enabled through Google Play services / Firebase in future updates, those services may receive anonymous technical diagnostics (app version, device model, crash stacks). This policy will be updated if additional analytics are introduced.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases such as “Remove Ads” are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your full payment card details. Google’s purchase records are subject to Google’s policies.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention

Local game data remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Advertising partners retain data according to their own policies.

Your choices

  • Uninstall the App to stop local processing.
  • Use Android advertising ID controls to limit ad personalization.
  • Contact us to ask privacy questions: adam.segev@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as the App or legal requirements change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
