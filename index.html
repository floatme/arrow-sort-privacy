This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application 2048 Neon (listed on Google Play as Neon 2048; Android package com.arrowsort.puzzle), published by PoofAI (“we”, “us”, or “the developer”). It explains what information is processed when you use the App on Google Play / Android.

Who we are

The App is published on Google Play by the developer account PoofAI. For privacy questions about this App, contact us at ollamaclient@gmail.com (the contact email shown on the Google Play store listing).

Information we collect and process

The App does not require you to create an account and does not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.

We process the following categories of data in connection with the App:

  • Local game data (on your device): progress such as scores, best score, daily streak, undo balances, board mode preferences, and whether you purchased “Remove Ads”. This data stays on your device unless you clear app data or uninstall.
  • Advertising / device data (via Google AdMob and partners): when ads are enabled, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and interactions with ads in order to serve, measure, and (where permitted) personalize advertising.
  • Purchase data (via Google Play): if you buy “Remove Ads” or other in-app products, Google Play processes the transaction. We receive purchase entitlement status (for example, that ads were removed). We do not receive your full payment card details.

Advertising

Unless you purchase Remove Ads, the App displays ads served by Google AdMob. Google’s processing of advertising-related data is described in Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob help.

On Android, you can reset or limit your advertising ID in system settings. In regions that require it (such as the EEA/UK/Switzerland), a consent form may be shown before personalized ads are requested.

Analytics and crash diagnostics

The App may receive basic technical diagnostics through Google Play services (for example app version, device model, or crash information) to keep the App stable. We do not use a separate third-party analytics SDK beyond Google Play / AdMob as described here. If we add additional analytics later, this policy will be updated before or when that change ships.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases such as “Remove Ads” are processed by Google Play Billing. Google’s handling of payment information is governed by Google’s policies. Purchase status may be stored locally on your device and restored through Play.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear storage for the App or uninstall it. Advertising and purchase partners retain data according to their own policies. To stop local processing, uninstall the App. For privacy requests related to this App, email ollamaclient@gmail.com.

Your choices

  • Uninstall the App to stop local processing on your device.
  • Use Android advertising ID controls to limit ad personalization.
  • Purchase Remove Ads (where offered) to stop showing ads in the App.
  • Contact ollamaclient@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Changes
